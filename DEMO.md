## Product pitch

Study Sprout helps middle-school students turn overwhelming homework into small study steps and grow a garden through completed work and checks for understanding. The first target is students who need repeated help starting homework; parents are the initial buyer hypothesis. The proposed benefit is greater study independence and fewer reminders. Customer demand, willingness to pay, and learning outcomes still need validation.
